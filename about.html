Who We Are

JobInMinute is a sister concern and is proudly powered by Kinetics Source Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a company built on the mission of connecting technical talent with the right opportunities, quickly and reliably. We bring together job seekers and employers on one platform, removing the friction typically involved in hiring and job hunting.

For Job Seekers

Looking for your next opportunity? Create your profile, upload your resume, and apply to relevant job openings in just a few clicks. Track your applications, get discovered by employers actively hiring, and take the next step in your career — all in one place, completely free for candidates.

For Employers

Hiring made simple. Post your job openings and get discovered by qualified candidates actively looking for work. Browse profiles, review resumes, and connect directly with potential hires — at no cost. Whether you need one role filled or are building an entire team, JobInMinute helps you find the right people, fast.
